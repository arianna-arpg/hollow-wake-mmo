02 / COMBAT GRAMMAR

Read the act before the ornament.

Choose a Thread. Compare every phase with colour removed and the fight crowded.

The threat teeth and support squares keep their meaning across every Thread. Decorative wisps yield to these signs.

03 / THE LAND ANSWERS

A Holding reads as care.

One place, four states. Crop order, joining strands and broken ties reveal what changed.

Demonstration values are authored for this study. Binding progress, ownership, contest state, decree effects and resource availability must come from server state in a client. The contour shown here is an inspectable boundary aid, not a permanent luminous wall.

MATERIAL

Stone holds. Cloth yields.

Broad painted planes and worn edges establish the world. Fine etching belongs near the player, in artifacts and the interface.

MAGIC

Thread has a grain.

Shape and cadence carry identity. Hue adds richness. Umbra settles; Void removes. Neither is a moral alignment.

TRUTH

A cue is a promise.

Only confirmed hits bloom at contact. An interrupted cast unthreads inward. No client guesses success from a vanished bar.
