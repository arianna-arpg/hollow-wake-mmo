# Weaves and effects

A weave is what a character casts. Players compose weaves; presets exist for monsters and starting kits.

## Composition

`WeaveSpec` = a preset id, or a **form** plus one or two **Threads** plus up to four **glyphs** (limits per form). Glyph order is socket order. A glyph reference carries a level and, for glyphs with rolled axes, the row each axis rolled: `fork`, `fork@3`, or `{ id: fork, level: 3, rows: { axis: row } }`. A reference without a level takes the level the glyph declares; levels are capped by rule `glyphs.max_level`. A reference without a row on an axis takes that axis's first row, the canonical roll of granted copies.

- the form supplies targeting, cast mode, timing and cost expressions, local attribute bases (`base_damage`, `area.radius`, `projectile.count`, `range`, `duration`, ...), declared mechanisms (projectile, area, duration, summon, chain, ...) and an effect graph
- a preset names its form, Threads and glyphs and adds its own attributes (bases that win over the form's), modifiers, effects (joining the root after the form's), tags and the tag `preset.{id}`, declared mechanisms, an AI hint, a cooldown clock, held-mode blocks of its own and the casting behaviors below; a shell form that reads its timing and costs from attributes (`cast_time: weave.cast.time`) lets many presets of one delivery share it
- Threads add their ids and tags and feed `$threads` and `$thread_status` in the graph
- the compiler adds a cast-scope tag from the cast mode and base cast time: `cast.instant`, `cast.timed`, `cast.channel` or `cast.held`, so modifiers can scope by commitment
- glyphs are gated by tags (any of `requires`, none of `excludes`) and by mechanisms (all of `requires_mechanisms`, plus those of their rolled rows), then add modifiers (their own, their rows', and `per_level` scaled by the glyph's level minus one), tags, mechanisms, effects at the root, hooks and patches; `level_bonus` and `level_bonus_per_level` raise the weave's level
- the result is a `CompiledWeave` with a tag set, mechanisms (declared and probed), bases, modifiers, compiled effects, costs, the held-mode blocks it holds (`Channel`, `Charge`, `Guard`, `Timing`), the patched graph it compiled from (`Specs`), its active glyph references and its dormant glyphs

### The fixpoint and dormant glyphs

Glyphs can enable each other: one adds a tag, a mechanism, a cooldown or a projectile another needs. The compiler starts with every glyph active, builds the weave (tags, mechanisms, modifiers, the patched and hooked graph, then the probes), and checks every active glyph's gate. The last one in socket order that fails goes dormant with its reason and the weave is rebuilt without it, until every active gate holds. Only removals happen, so this ends, and the result does not depend on socket order except where glyphs exclude each other, where the earlier socket stays active.

On a weave with the mechanism rule `minions.forward_requires` names (a summoning that carries a resonant key), a forwarding glyph whose gate fails rides to the crew instead of going dormant: its `forwards` flag says whether it forwards (by default it does unless it carries a trigger), and the glyph granting the mechanism never rides. A rider is listed in `CompiledWeave.Riding`, gives the weave nothing but its cost modifiers (`cost` and the `cost.*` families), which the weave pays, and boards the minions' weaves as the minion contract describes; once the mechanism is lost, riders are misfits again. A strict compile accepts a rider.

A dormant glyph stays socketed and contributes nothing, not even costs. `WeaveCompiler.Compile(spec, GlyphFit.Dormant)` compiles that way and lists dormant glyphs with their reasons; `GlyphFit.Strict` (the default) refuses a weave with a misfit, naming the first one. Presets and starting weaves in content must fit: validation compiles each and warns when a glyph would be dormant.

### Hooks

A glyph's `hooks` map hook names to effect lists. Effect types declare which of their lists run at which named moment through their `EffectSignature`: a projectile's `on_hit`, an area's or chain's per-target `effects` and a field's `pulse` are `on_hit`; a damage effect's `on_kill` runs when its hit kills. The compiler appends each glyph's hook list, in glyph order, into every node of the weave's graph that exposes the hook, wherever it sits in the graph; `on_cast` lists join the root list and run once each time the weave resolves. Effects a hook adds are never themselves hooked. A hook name no registered type exposes fails validation.

### Patches

A glyph's `patches` edit the parameters of the form's and preset's effect nodes before compiling, in glyph order: `{ type, where, set, wrap, mode }`. `type` names the effect type to match; `where` is `any` (default), `root` (nodes that were in the root list, even once wrapped) or `first` (the first match walking the graph). `set` assigns parameters; `wrap` replaces the matched node with a wrapper effect holding it in the list the wrapper declares (`effects` for `repeat`, `sequence` and `delay`). Modes:

- `first` (the default for `set`): the first glyph to set a parameter wins over the form and over later glyphs
- `default`: sets a parameter only where the node has none
- `append`: appends list values to the node's lists
- `wrap` (the default for a patch with only `wrap`): every glyph wraps, each wrapper directly around the node, so the first glyph's wrapper ends outermost; in `first` or `default` mode a node is wrapped only once per wrapper type

Validation rejects a patch naming an unknown type, a parameter the type does not declare, a wrapper that is not one, or an append with a value that is not a list. Choices are patches; quantities are attributes.

### Trees

A form may carry a `tree` of nodes a weave buys with points, one per rank: rule `casting.tree_points`, an expression of the weave's own level, says how many points it has, and nothing can be bought below the tree's `opens_at`. `WeaveSpec.Tree` lists the spent nodes in the order they were bought, a node once per rank. Walking that list, a node counts when it is in the tree with a rank left, one of its `links` was bought before it (a node without links is a root), nothing bought excludes it either way round (`excludes`), and a point is left. A strict compile fails on the first node that does not count; the dormant fit that player slots use keeps it spent but dormant, with its reason (`CompiledWeave.DormantNodes`), so later nodes that only linked through it go dormant too. Each rank of a counting node adds its `modifiers` once more under source `tree:{node}`; from its first rank its `patches` edit the graph after the glyphs' patches (so a socketed glyph's choice wins over the weave's own tree) and its `glyph` joins as a slot graft does, beyond the sockets. Validation checks that links and excludes name nodes of the same tree, that the tree has a root and that every node can be reached from one. `WeaveRuntime.ResetTree` clears a seat's tree, giving its points back, for a station's reset service; `TreePoints` reads what a seat spent and has.

### Mechanisms and probes

A weave's mechanisms are the ones its form and active glyphs declare plus every probe that holds on it. Probes are pure functions over the weave being compiled (its patched graph, form timing, bases and modifiers) registered by name in `MechanismRegistry`; each area registers its own in `Weaves/Probes/`. A required mechanism may carry a parameter after a colon (`affliction:bleeding`, `surface:standing`, `flight:children`); the probe receives it, and an unknown probe or an unacceptable parameter fails validation. Plain names that are not probes are declared mechanisms and stay open. Probes never name weave ids. So a glyph that levies `cooldown.added` lifts a glyph that needs `cooldown`, and a Thread whose ailment is burning lets a weave meet `affliction:burning`. A probe of a quantity a node may write reads it through the door its handler reads (`WeaveProbe.Holds`): the node's own parameter where the node sets it, else the weave attribute the parameter falls back to (named by `ProbeBindings` from the same axis bindings the composition lint reads), the weave's base folded with its own modifiers; a value that acts either way round counts whatever its sign. A quantity no node writes (a weave's repeats, its leech, its bank of uses, a levied cooldown) has only its attribute door, and its probe asks whether the weave itself raises it (`WeaveProbe.Raises`). So a glyph gated on `flight:spends`, as `returning` is, needs a flight its hits spend, and stays dormant on a weave whose flights never end on a hit, such as a wheel's orbiting blades.

The report and composition lint share `Weaves/AxisBindings.cs` beside `EffectSignature`: it declares each parameter's dimension, units and attribute fallback, multiplier or channel. A written damage effectiveness shadows `damage.effectiveness` for added damage and also scales the written amount; when absent, the report places only the implicit added-damage axis. The glyph census tries compatible forms and then presets until graph contributions produce values, so an empty shell cannot hide a hook's literals.

## Runtime

Players, monsters and summons cast through one `WeaveRuntime`. Slots compile through `Resolve` with misfit glyphs dormant (their reasons stay on `WeaveSlot.Dormant`), so a seat holding a misfit still casts; presets in content must fit. `SetWeaveIntent` compiles the same way; while rule `casting.require_ownership` holds it asks the registered `IWeaveOwnership` (a missing service is permission), and changing the loadout follows rule `casting.swap_requires`, an expression over the caster waived where the ground carries the tag rule `casting.swap_waived_by` names. Swapping rack seats is always free.

### Beginning a cast

`TryBegin` validates in one place, in this order: alive; a toggle that is on turns off and a triggered weave's key arms or disarms it (neither casts); not stunned or frozen; the face (below) carries no forbidden tag (statuses set `forbid:{tag}` flags; the flags a weave checks are built once per compiled weave); not busy, unless the face is an instant reflex (form `reflex`, or attribute `reflex` above zero), which resolves past the running cast without touching it; off its cooldown and the caster's global lock (rule `combat.global_cooldown` after every cast, and a form's `recovery`); a use in its bank; its gates; the charges its spend requires; affordable; a valid target. Then a fizzle may claim it (attribute `cast.fizzle_chance`: the costs are paid, the cast fails and the caster takes rule `casting.fizzle_status` for `casting.fizzle_duration`); otherwise it pays, spends a use from its bank, starts its cooldown when that is clocked at the press, and resolves at once or begins its hold.

The face is what a press casts now: a convert's preset while its `when` holds (an expression over the caster with the slot's state as `slot.*`, such as `slot.uses < 1`), else the next step of a combo `chain` pressed within its window, else the slot's own weave. A face compiles at the slot weave's level, keeps its own cooldown and costs, and runs with the host slot as its context's slot, so a restring converted onto an empty quiver refills that quiver's bank.

A form's `target` block names a registered target kind (`TargetKinds`: `enemy`, `ally` and `any` are built in; packages register corpses, minions and the like), an optional condition over `target.*`, a search radius (the weave's range by default), a fallback (`fail`, `self` or the bare `aim`) and whether the weave consumes what it targets (the kind's owner reads it). The given body must fit; otherwise the kind searches near the aim; otherwise the fallback decides, all before any cost is paid.

Forms, presets and glyphs all carry the casting behaviors below. Where a weave holds only one of a kind (a bank of uses, a spend, a convert, a cycle, a self stack, a chain), the first active glyph in socket order wins over later glyphs, the preset over the form; triggers, gates, metas, follow-ups and worn modifiers add up, the form's first, then the preset's, then the glyphs' in socket order. Forms and presets, not glyphs, also carry the held-mode blocks (`channel`, `charge`, `guard` and `timing`, under Holding a cast). A preset's block replaces its form's whole, never field by field, so what the preset says is what the weave does, its release and bash included; the cast mode stays the form's, and validation refuses a preset block that mode would not read (a `channel` on a form that is not a channel, and likewise `charge` and `guard`; `timing` on anything but a cast bar). The compiled weave's `Channel`, `Charge`, `Guard` and `Timing` are the blocks it holds, and the runtime, the mechanism probes and the compiled sections read only those; `hollowwake weave` prints each block a weave carries and where it came from.

Costs are the form's per-resource formulas plus `cost.added.{resource}` (so a glyph can make a weave cost a resource it did not), through the caster's `cost` fold scoped by `cost.{resource}` and the weave's tags, then moved in part by `cost.convert.{from}.{to}`. The families are read only when the weave or the caster's stack touches them. A payment stamps the `last_cost.{resource}` gauges when the weave reads them or rule `casting.stamp_costs` holds. `gates` on forms and glyphs are expressions over the caster that must all hold. A `spend` takes `amount` (or `all`) of a named charge when the use resolves, at least its `minimum` unless `optional`; each charge spent adds its `scales` as cast-local modifiers and the count is the variable `charges_spent`.

Cast time divides by `attack.speed` for attacks or `cast.speed` for everything else, and so do a channel block's `interval` and `windup`, a brim's fill time, a charge-up's time to full and an overcharge's stage time. A cooldown is the form's value plus `cooldown.added` (a glyph can levy one, which lifts glyphs that need the `cooldown` mechanism), divided by `cooldown.recovery`, keyed by the weave. Its clock starts at the press or when the cast resolves, as the preset's `cooldown_at`, else the form's, else rule `combat.cooldown_at` says; a channel with `cooldown_on_end` starts it when the channel ends. A weave with a bank of uses keeps no cooldown between uses. A form's `recovery` holds its caster from beginning anything else for that long after it resolves.

### Holding a cast

A cast in progress is a `CastState`: its slot and the weave it resolves (the face), its mode, target, focus (the body a held cast turns toward without an aim of its own), point and direction, timing, and how it is held (`Held`, `Fill`, `Stage` of `Stages`, `Brim`), the live aim mirrored from the weave book, and modifiers local to the cast. `MoveFactor` is the fraction of movement speed the caster keeps: one for weaves tagged `mobile`, else the mode's own `move` (a channel's or a guard's), the form's `cast_move` or rule `casting.cast_move`, plus attribute `cast.mobility`, clamped to zero..one; the movement system multiplies speed by it. The casting system advances every book's cast each tick, in the world's order; a stun, a freeze or a forbidden tag interrupts, and death clears it. A hold ends by `ReleaseWeaveIntent` (`Release`), by its own limit, by running dry, or by interruption. Every end but an interruption publishes `WeaveReleased` with its reason and whether its payload fired; an interruption fires nothing and publishes `WeaveInterrupted`, though a hold that already took effect (a channel that beat, a raised guard) still starts the cooldown it held for its end.

- **Channel.** After its windup, a channel beats every `interval` (with `jitter` drawn from the cast's own random stream). The press paid the first beat; each later beat pays the form's costs, and one it cannot pay ends the channel (`dry`, which releases). Each beat builds its `ramps`: a value per held second shaped by a named curve and capped by `max` or floored by `min`; ramps on the weave become cast-local modifiers joining that beat's folds (through a view of the compiled weave carrying them, so in-flight effects keep their own beat's values), ramps on the caster sit on the caster under source `cast:{slot}` until the cast ends. A beat runs the form's graph with `cast.held` (unless its release says `pulses: false`: such a channel gathers silently and fires only on release), publishes `ChannelPulsed` and raises trigger kind `channel_beat`. With `track_aim` it turns toward the live aim at `turn_rate` degrees per second. It ends at `max_hold` (the form's cast time for a channel without one, else rule `casting.max_hold`). Its `release` fires its effects (or the form's graph) with the ramps at the held time, after `min_hold` and, with `require_full`, only on a full hold; `persist` keeps the graph beating for `per_held_second` seconds per second held (at most `max_duration`), its damage fading to `fade`, following the caster. A `brim` is a gauge kept in the slot between channels (and mirrored into the caster's gauge `brim.{seat}`): holding fills it over `fill_time` (its longest hold without one), at rest it loses `decay` per second but never falls below `bank_at` once it reached it, a release below `min_release` fizzles, a release `spend`s it, `auto_release` lets go when it is full, and its `scale` shapes the release's damage from `at_empty` to `at_full`. Every channel end raises `channel_end`.
- **Charge-up.** The bar fills over `max_time`; a release (by intent, or by itself `full_hold` seconds after filling, rule `casting.full_hold` by default) resolves the weave with each `scales` entry as a cast-local modifier from `at_zero` to `at_full` by the fill, and with `cast.fill` and `cast.held`.
- **Overcharge.** The bar climbs a stage every `time`, up to `stages` (the base of attribute `overcharge.stages`, which glyphs raise), each paying `cost_per_stage` (a stage it cannot pay for is not reached), publishing `OverchargeStaged` and raising `overcharge_stage`. A release resolves the weave with `per_stage` modifiers scaled by the stage reached and `cast.stage`; released within the spark's `window` of a stage, it adds the spark's `bonus` as more damage. It releases by itself after its full hold at the last stage.
- **Guard.** Raising a guard runs the form's graph once and puts a `Guard` on its caster: a frontal `arc`, a `pool` scaled by attribute `guard.strength`, a `parry` window, flag `guarding`, a `turn_rate`, a `pulse` (a preset cast every `interval`, the beat of rule `casting.channel_interval` by default, raising `guard_beat`) and a longest `max_duration`. The `guard` hit stage, before evasion, catches hits whose source lies inside the arc: in the parry window a hit is parried whole (`GuardParried`, trigger kind `parry`; with `end_on_parry` the guard then ends), afterwards the pool takes what it can, a hit taken whole is published as blocked, and an empty pool breaks the guard (`GuardBroken`). When the guard ends its `bash` fires if it was armed (held past `arm_time` with `threshold` of the pool absorbed), or on a break with `on_break`, with `guard.absorbed` and `cast.held`.
- **Strike timing.** A cast bar with `timing` reads a second press (`PressWeaveIntent`): `perfect` lands flawless in the bar's last `window` seconds, `timed` in a `window` centered on a marker drawn from the cast's stream, and a flawless press adds `bonus` as more damage and raises `flawless`; `multitude` strikes once per press, up to `window` presses. Every press publishes `WeavePressed`.

The live aim arrives by `AimIntent`: kept on the weave book (`AimPoint`, `AimDirection`, `AimAt`) when it comes no faster than rule `casting.max_aim_rate` per second, pulled within rule `casting.max_aim_range` of the caster, and mirrored on a running cast. Tracking channels and guards turn toward it, held casts aim along it when they fire, and metas aim at it. Release, press and aim that find nothing held from their seat are ignored.

### Resolving a use

A use resolves when an instant or timed cast resolves, when a charge-up or overcharge releases, and when a triggered weave casts. It spends its charges, adds its cast-local modifiers (self stacks, an empowering use, a flawless press, charge scales) through a view of the compiled weave, runs the graph with an `EffectContext` (its slot and host set, its random stream drawn from the caster, the weave, the tick and the cast's beat), publishes `WeaveCast`, and remembers the cast in the book's ring of recent casts (`Recent`, `RecentlyRepeated`, `RecentlyVaried`; rule `casting.recent_casts`). Then the slot's behaviors follow:

- **Repeats** from attribute `repeat.count` (fractions carry to the next use), plus unleash seals banked while the slot rested (`repeat.seals`, one per `repeat.seal_time`), plus one per extra use a bank vented with `steps_from_bank`, at most rule `casting.max_repeats`. Each repeat reruns the graph after `repeat.interval`, one deeper, with `repeat.index`, its areas larger by `repeat.scale` per repeat, turned to the nearest foe with `repeat.retarget`; `repeat.lock` holds the caster until they end. Each publishes `WeaveRepeated`.
- **Follow-ups** (`follow_up`, from the form and every active glyph): a preset cast `delay` seconds later with a `chance`, free, at the use's target or point.
- **Cycles** run their effects every `count`-th use. **Self stacks** grow one per use to `max` and empower the weave with their `modifiers` per stack; the pile drops after `duration` without a use, or peels a stack every `decay`.
- **Toggles** turn on: a toggle's `reserve` writes flat modifiers on `{resource}.reserved` (the usable maximum subtracts them) under the toggle's source and its flag `toggle.{form}` goes on; the upkeep system pays its `upkeep` each tick, growing by `ramp` of itself per second on, and turns it off when its bearer starves, its `max_duration` passes, its slot leaves the rack or its bearer dies, running `on_deactivate`. Pressing it again turns it off. Each change publishes `WeaveToggled`.

A **bank of uses** (`uses` on the form or on a glyph) starts full at `uses.max` (its `max` as base) and lives in the slot's state: a trickle bank brings a use back every `recharge` seconds (the weave's cooldown by default, divided by attribute `uses.rate`); a `magazine` refills all at once when emptied, or a use at a time with `drip`; a `still` bank recovers only while its caster stands still and bleeds while it moves; a `manual` bank never recovers on its own, so only `restore_uses` and converts refill it (munitions reloaded by hand); `vent_all` spends the whole bank on one press (`uses_spent`); `empower` makes uses optional fuel that adds its modifiers per use spent. `restore_uses` and converts reload banks. Changes publish `UsesChanged`. Reading a bank changes nothing: what it holds now is computed from what it held when it last changed, and it is brought up to date only as it changes (a spend, a restore, a still bank's beat). The other reads a slot message makes (`Uses`, `FaceOf`, `Stacks`, `MetasOf`, `TreePoints`) are pure too, so a snapshot never moves the simulation; a convert's `slot.uses`, `slot.stack` and `slot.brim` read the live values the same way, and a lapsed combo chain shows its first face again without resetting until the next press walks it.

**Metas** (`meta_action`, from the form and then each active glyph in socket order) run by `UseMetaIntent`, one beat apart (rule `casting.meta_interval`), each cast like the `cast` effect with the slot as host. **Worn modifiers** (`equip_modifiers`, from the form and every active glyph) sit on the caster under source `weave:{seat}` while the weave is slotted. **Grafts** put glyphs on whatever weave sits in a seat: attribute family `glyph.slot{seat}.{glyph}` (seats count from one) gives the glyph's level; a socketed copy wins, a misfit graft goes dormant, and grafts do not count against the form's sockets. The runtime arms a book whenever its slots change, and whenever its bearer's grafts change from anywhere (gear, passives, statuses): like minion inheritance, the casting system watches the bearer's attribute version and compares the grafts only when the bearer carries the graft family or the book holds grafts. Arming compiles every seat, puts worn modifiers on, reads the grafts and subscribes the triggered seats.

### Triggers

A `trigger` on a form or a glyph makes its weave cast itself when an event of the named trigger kind happens to its caster; the slot's key then arms and disarms it, and the AI never presses it. One event casts at most one triggered seat of its caster, tried round-robin down the bar from the seat after the last that fired; a seat fires when it is armed, its internal cooldown (`icd`) has passed, the event carries one of its `tags` and its `status`, enough damage taken accumulated (`life_fraction` of maximum life, for damage-taken triggers), its weave is quick enough (base cast time at most `max_cast_time`, else rule `triggers.max_cast_time`, unless mechanism `trigger_permit` rides along), it is off cooldown, affordable and its gates hold, and its chance roll succeeds: attribute `trigger.chance` with the trigger's `chance` as base, capped by rule `triggers.chance_cap`, rolled from the caster's own trigger stream. Held modes, toggles and weaves tagged `untriggerable` never trigger. A triggered cast resolves at once at the event's other party (the struck body for the caster's hits, the attacker for hits on it; the caster for self and ally weaves), one level deeper than the event, paying its costs and cooldown honestly, with `trigger.amount` (the event's amount) and `trigger.power` (its `power`, an expression over the caster that may read the event's `amount`); events at rule `triggers.max_depth` or deeper trigger nothing. It publishes `WeaveTriggered` and `WeaveCast`. The kinds the cast modes raise are `channel_beat`, `channel_end`, `release`, `overcharge_stage`, `flawless`, `guard_beat` and `parry`.

AI chooses weaves through the same runtime with `ChooseForAi`, using each form's `ai` hint and range, skipping triggered weaves, toggles that are on, empty banks and failed gates. A hint's `min_range` is a soft minimum: closer than it (edge to edge) the weave weighs `near_weight` instead of `weight`, and without a near weight it waits for room. `PreferredRange`, which positions the AI, reads each hint's `preferred_range`, or the weave's folded `range_attribute` when the hint names one, so the range scales with the caster.

## Effects

The runtime also publishes confirmed semantic cast lifecycle events. Effect contexts retain the cast identity through delayed and nested delivery copies so confirmed damage can be correlated after a cast ends. The client-only recipe and event contract is [cast-presentation.md](cast-presentation.md).

A registry of handlers compiles typed specs into executable effects. Handlers are grouped in families, one file each under `Effects/Handlers/` with a static `Register(EffectRegistry)`: damage and healing, statuses, charges, status transfer, deliveries, placements, movement, summons, minions, AI, control flow, casting, utilities. `BuiltinEffects.RegisterAll` only calls the families; a new handler goes into its family's file. Each handler reads its parameters as expressions, so `amount: "weave.base_damage * spell.power"` is ordinary, and declares an `EffectSignature`: the parameters it reads, the lists that are hooks, and for wrappers the list a wrap patch fills. Validation reads the signatures in every kind's effect lists (weave graphs and sections, glyph lists, status lists and hooks, procs, dialogue effects), nested lists included wherever a parameter holds objects naming a `type`: it warns about a parameter a type does not read and about an unknown type in a nested list, and errs on an unknown type at the top of a list. A signature also declares which of its parameters name content (`Refers`: a status, a charge kind, a pattern), and validation errs on a name no definition carries, leaving `$` variables to the compiler; the words a `filter` may hold are a registry (`Filters`), so an unknown word fails validation and reaches nobody at run time. A signature may also declare checks of its own (`Checking`), and validation errs on a node one refuses: a `resource` effect lowering life by a literal amount, or a `heal` of a negative one, since life falls only through the damage ladder and a decrease of it is a `damage` effect (a heal only restores, and one its healing fold takes below zero heals nothing).

The context carries depth; nested deliveries, repeats, delays and casts deepen it, and rule `effects.max_depth` caps recursion. Damage, healing, death and status events carry the depth they happened at. `last_damage` and `amount` are set by damage effects so a following `apply_status` can size an ailment from the hit.

### Casting another weave, or the host again

`cast { weave, at, aim, turn, level, damage_scale }` runs a preset's effect graph from inside another graph: sequels where a flight ends, emitters, follow-ups, contagion releases. The preset compiles once per level. It runs free of cost and cooldown, raises no `WeaveCast`, one level deeper than its host, with the host's weave level (or `level`), the host's tags joined to its own, and the host's origin for ownership and credit. `at` is `point` (default: the context's point), `target` or `caster`; at a point or a target its deliveries start there rather than at the caster's body. `aim` is `onward` (default: the host's direction, else out from the host's center), `out`, `random` (from the cast's random stream) or `rotating` (turning by `turn` degrees, default 45, each time the effect runs for the same host). `damage_scale` scales its damage effects only.

`weave: host` names no preset but the host itself, the weave whose graph is running (the context's `Weave`: inside a preset a `cast` runs, that preset, not the slot's weave `Host` names). The host is read when the effect runs, so a glyph's hook that casts the host re-runs whatever weave it was compiled into: contagion re-releases, curse-on-hit strikes and fields that re-afflict are hooks. It runs the host's root list again, `on_cast` lists included, as this cast of the host stands (its level, its tags, its cast-local modifiers), with `at`, `aim` and `damage_scale` as above, free and one level deeper; `level` fails compilation, and where no weave runs (a status's or a proc's effects) it does nothing. A cast of the host is refused inside rule `effects.host_depth` host casts of the same weave, a count the context's variables keep per weave down every delivery: at one, a weave whose hit casts itself lands that hit once more and its rerun casts nothing further, long before `effects.max_depth` would cut the chain, and the host casts of a preset it casts keep their own count. Validation refuses a preset named `host`.

### Delay

`delay { seconds, effects }` schedules its list through the simulation scheduler, at least one tick later, one level deeper, with a detached copy of the context: it reads the stats of the moment it fires and its own copy of the variables. Nothing fires once its source no longer exists.

### Fuses, cooldowns, banks and guards

`guard { amount, self }` surges a raised guard, adding `amount` to its pool, past the pool it was raised with if need be: the target's guard, or the source's with `self` or without a target; a body holding no guard, or a broken one, is untouched, and a surge publishes `GuardSurged`. A reflex weave gated on the `guarding` flag pours resources into a held guard this way.

`fuse { delay, power, effects }` is a wrapper that banks each run of its list and resolves it after `delay` (attribute `fuse.delay` in the weave's scope by default), one level deeper, at the caster's stats of that moment, its damage scaled by one plus `power` (attribute `fuse.power` by default); glyphs wrap a delivery's hits in it with a `wrap` patch. `cooldown { seconds, fraction, scope }` shaves the running cooldowns of the source's weaves, first `seconds` off and then `fraction` of what remains; `scope` is `others` (default: all but the running weave), `all`, or a list of tags. `restore_uses { amount, scope }` puts use charges back into the host slot's bank (default), every bank (`all`) or the still banks (`still`); without an amount it fills them.

### The context's scope and host

An effect context's scope is built when first read: `source.*` and `caster.*` resolve on the source, `target.*` on the target, `weave.*` through the weave folded on the source with the context's tags, the variables as they stand when read (so what an effect writes, such as a damage effect's `last_damage` and `amount`, reaches the effects after it in its list), and `distance`, `depth` and `time` as they stood when it was built; every other name resolves on the source. A context carries the slot a cast came from (`Slot`) and, for metas, follow-ups, guard pulses and `cast`, the weave of that slot (`Host`), so effects that act on their host read `Host ?? Weave`. The cast variables a runtime sets are `cast.held`, `cast.fill`, `cast.stage`, `cast.persist`, `charges_spent`, `uses_spent`, `trigger.power`, `trigger.amount`, `repeat.index` and `guard.absorbed`; a formula reading one where it was never set fails like any unknown name.
