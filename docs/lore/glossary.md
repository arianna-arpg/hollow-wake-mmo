# Glossary

Every proper noun and term in the Hollow Wake lore, one line each, alphabetical by the main word. People are listed by their second name (house, by-name, wake-house or seam-name), except the Loosed, who have only one. The example name lists in naming.md are not glossed; everything that appears in the lore itself is.

## A

- Absent, the: the Mending Houses' name for the Loomwright, whose liturgy is built around an empty chair.
- Alderheart: a Bloom Fount in the roots of an ancient alder grove in the Sallows, bound by the Order of the Mended Thread, who raised the Hospice around it.
- Anchor, the: a Glyph that holds a Weave in place and makes it last.
- Anvil, the: an island of the Scatter, named for its look from a boat.
- Ash: a Loosed Weaver, a Marcher girl from Reedham who went into the Fray at fourteen and came back at twenty; the one the Compact fears most.
- Ashdown: a stead in the Hallows near Ninewells; its bottom-field has a Hob in it.
- Ashdown, Hob: Stone-weaver of the Nine Who Stayed; the first well at Ninewells bears his name.
- Ashfall: an Ember Weave, one of the Kindler's first.
- ashlight: frayed Ember; cold fire that gives no warmth, consumes nothing, and spreads.
- ashwolf: one of the Tangled, a wolf re-tied with Ember.
- Aurevant: the Concord house of Lumen, which founded Cassevault and kept its name through the Loose Years.
- Aurevant, Aurelis: a Wellsinger of the Hem coast who sat the first Vigil, bound Font Aurelle and raised Cassevault.
- Aurevant, Lucan: Clerk of the Lantern Chancery who keeps the Candle at Ninewells and is polite to everyone.
- Aureth: the world.

## B

- Barrow at Fallow's Cross, the: a Barrow-knot in the Hallows; the Snarl every new Weaver at Ninewells is sent to first.
- Barrow-knot: a Hallows Snarl in a burial mound, Stone and Umbra, where something laid down carefully has woken.
- barrowman: one of the Tangled, risen from a barrow.
- Belcastre: the Concord house of Mind; its name was struck from every Lantern in Ilvary after the Unraveling and is borne by no one.
- Belcastre, Serapha: Chancellor of the Ninth Hearth, who proposed the Great Working in C 1180.
- Binder: the Calling of those who bind Patterns to their own; Mind and Lumen, with Bloom or Umbra.
- binding: the act of tying a Weaver's Skein into a Fount's Pattern by the Vigil, so that the Fount answers to them.
- blightbloom: frayed Bloom; growth without pattern, or its mirror, barrenness.
- Bloom: the Thread of life and growth, healing and birth.
- Bloomspan: the fourth Span of the year, the growing.
- Bobbin, the (moon): the Marcher name for the moon, which winds and unwinds.
- Bobbin, the (Glyph): a Glyph that stores a Weave and lets it go later.
- Bulwark: a Stone Weave, one of the Holdfast's first.
- Burl: a Knot that has set: hardened, stable, rich in Thread, and rare.
- Burl floss: the rare floss shed by a Burl.
- Burr, Lowdy: Heddle-mistress of Ninewells, who taught half the Reweavers in the Marches.
- Burr, the (Glyph): a Glyph that makes a Weave cling and linger.

## C

- Calling: the path a new Weaver starts on; a beginning, never a cage. There are six.
- Candle, the: the Lantern Chancery's office in a town; the one in Ninewells is tolerated.
- Candlehall: a Concord waystation on the Loomroad south of Ninewells, re-lit in M 287 and now the Chancery's seat in the Marches.
- Candlehall Font: a Lumen Fount beside Candlehall, bound by the Marchwarden under the Charter of Lanterns, which the Compact does not recognise.
- Cassevault: the capital of Ilvary, the city of lanterns, raised around Font Aurelle; seat of the Chancery.
- Cast, the: the third movement of weaving, letting a drawn Thread go into the world as a Weave.
- Castellane: the Concord house of Stone, which kept its name through the Loose Years.
- Castellane, Isaure: Chancellor and Marchwarden, the Chancery's voice in the Weftmarches, seated at Candlehall.
- Celandine, Sister: the Mender who keeps the Hospice-gate at Ninewells and teaches the Sower's Calling.
- Chancellor: a senior officer of the Lantern Chancery, seated at Cassevault.
- Chancery of Lanterns, the: the Concord-era name of the Lantern Chancery, founded in C 112 to keep the Fount charters.
- Chandevault: the Chancery's Concord name for Candlehall, which nobody in the Marches uses.
- Charter of Lanterns, the: the Chancery's law of M 93 that no Fount may be bound except by a sealed Weaver, and every binding is a loan from the Chancery.
- cinderhound: one of the Tangled, a hound re-tied with Ember, common in the Delves.
- Cinderedge: an Ember Weave, one of the Holdfast's first.
- Cinderstair, the: a Firstwork in the Emberfells; a staircase of fused black glass that climbs a scarp and stops in the air.
- Clerk: a Chancery officer who keeps a Candle.
- close folk, the close-woven: what Weavers call people who cannot weave; no insult is meant.
- close-wove: an item whose Pattern has no room for Glyphs.
- Coldedge: a Tide Weave, one of the Harrier's first.
- Cole, Edda: of Wenna Cole's line; keeps the smithy at Ninewells and teaches the Kindler's Calling.
- Cole, Wenna: Ember-weaver of the Nine Who Stayed.
- Common: the shared tongue of Aureth, spoken plainly by Marchers.
- Concord Age, the: the twelve centuries from the swearing of the Concord to the Unraveling.
- Concord Count, the: the old calendar, written C, counted from the swearing of the Concord of Nine Hearths.
- Concord of Nine Hearths, the: the pact of nine Weaver-houses sworn at Cassevault in C 1, which ruled Ilvary until the tear.
- Concord works: Landmarks made in the Concord Age: Lanterns, Loomroads, Vaults, Greyheron Light.
- cord: the knotted cord on which a Marcher Weaver records their Glyphs; also a Marcher knot-prayer.
- Crackle: a Storm Weave, one of the Kindler's first.
- Cross, the: a Glyph that binds two Threads into one Weave.
- Crow: a Harrier who has taken up Umbra; also, uncapitalised, a member of the Hedgecrows.
- crossing: a Weaver's growth from one Calling into another.
- Crowmother: the title of Kit Tallow, leader of the Hedgecrows.

## D

- Delve: an Emberfells Snarl, a collapsed working re-tangled into new galleries; also the Durn form for a Holding.
- Delve at Slagtooth, the: the deepest Delve known in the Emberfells.
- Delver: a Durn miner; a member of the Greyseam Delvers.
- Demesne: the Ilvarene form for a Holding, as in the Demesne of Font Aurelle.
- distaff: the Reweaver's tool that holds a Pattern open while it is unpicked.
- Draw, the: the second movement of weaving, taking hold of a Thread and pulling it toward a shape.
- Drawn, the: Weavers who died on Ninth Void when their Skeins were pulled out of them toward the east.
- Dropped Shuttle, the: the Loosed name for the Loomwright, who let go of the Loom on purpose.
- drowned, the: Tangled dead of the meres and sea-caves, re-tied with Tide; never given any other name.
- Drowned Font, the: a Tide Fount under the deepest mere of the Sallows, reachable only when the water chooses.
- Drowning: a Sallows Tide Snarl under a mere, which floods and empties on its own schedule.
- Durenne: the Concord house of Ember, which kept its name through the Loose Years.
- Durenne, Mathis: Lanterner, second to the Marchwarden, who likes the Marches more than his office allows.
- Durn: the people of the Girdle and Ondur; Stone's own, patient and blunt.
- Duskveil: an Umbra Weave, one of the Shade's first.

## E

- Eastmarch, the: the Concord's name for the country that became the Weftmarches.
- Echo, the (Glyph): a Glyph that makes a Weave repeat.
- Echo (Knot): a Hush Thinning of Mind, a place that replays what happened in it.
- Echo of Serapha, the: an Echo in the Hush that replays an hour of a woman in Concord dress speaking to no one.
- Ember: the Thread of fire and heat, change by consumption.
- Emberfells, the: the upland sub-region of the Weftmarches, Stone and Ember, with Storm on the heights.
- Emberlash: an Ember Weave, one of the Kindler's first.
- Emberspan: the fifth Span of the year, high summer.
- Eye: the heart of a Fount, where the Threads can be seen directly; a Wakefolk word now used everywhere.
- Eye of Greyheron, the: a Storm and Tide Fount at the foot of Greyheron Light, bound by the Light's keeper.

## F

- Factor, the: the head of the Longwake Company, seated at Lowkeel; lower-cased, any Company agent.
- Fall, the: the fourth movement of weaving, when a Weave ends and its Threads return to the Loom.
- Fallow, Piers: Bloom-weaver of the Nine Who Stayed.
- Fallow's Cross: a stead in the Hallows with a barrow beside it.
- Fenn, Jory: Hedge-captain of Ninewells, who teaches the Holdfast's Calling at the north gate.
- First Lantern, the: the Lantern raised at Cassevault in C 44, which guttered and held on Ninth Void and burns still.
- Firstdelve, Ysmur: the Durn who opened Scarrow Gate from the mountain side in C 290.
- Firstworks: Landmarks that were there before any people, made by the Loomwright or by nothing at all.
- floss: loose Thread shed by Knots, the raw material of Reweaving; named by Thread, as Ember floss.
- flosser: one who harvests Knots for floss; the Company's crews, and independents who die young.
- Fold, the: whatever lies beyond the Girdle; uncrossed.
- Font: the Ilvarene word for a Fount, used before its name.
- Font Aurelle: the Lumen Fount beneath Cassevault, the first ever bound.
- Fork, the: a Glyph that splits a Weave.
- Fount: a wellspring where the Loom surfaces and can be touched; bind one and the land around it answers to you.
- Fray, the: the torn country in the east where the Unraveling opened; it has no fixed shape and the Unmade come out of it.
- fray-floss: Void floss brought out of the Fray by the Loosed and sold to the Company.
- fraying: what a Thread does when it goes wrong; each Thread frays in its own way.

## G

- Gale, Bram: Storm-weaver of the Nine Who Stayed.
- Gale, Dunstan: of Bram Gale's line; teaches the Harrier's Calling on the river path at Ninewells.
- Galecry: a Storm Weave, one of the Harrier's first.
- Girdle, the: the mountain range across the north of Aureth; the Durn say it is the hem that held.
- glassling: one of the Tangled, a creature gone over to pure Lumen structure.
- Glyph: a bound shape that changes how a Weave behaves; named for shapes, tied into a Weave.
- Great Working, the: the working of all nine houses at Cassevault on Ninth Void, C 1211, whose purpose is disputed and whose day was the Unraveling's.
- Greyheron: a Wakefolk wake-house that keeps Greyheron Light and holds Saltheart.
- Greyheron Light: a Concord Lantern built as a lighthouse on a sea-stack off the Saltwrack in C 731; its beam has never missed a night.
- Greyheron Stack: the hollow sea-stack on which the Light stands, with Saltheart in a cave beneath.
- Greyheron's Rest: an island of the Scatter.
- Greyseam, Haldrek: Seam-master of the Greyseam Delvers at Scarrow.
- Greyseam Delvers, the: a Durn kin at Scarrow who hold the Greyseam Heart, the oldest binding in the Marches.
- Greyseam Heart, the: a Stone Fount deep in the Durn workings below Scarrow, held through the tear and since.
- Grieve, Sable: Umbra-weaver of the Nine Who Stayed; her line has ended.

## H

- Halfmoon: an island of the Scatter.
- Hall of the Nine Hearths, the: the hall at Cassevault where the Great Working was performed and which was found empty in the morning.
- Hallows, the: the valley of the Wend, the settled heart of the Weftmarches, Bloom and Stone; Ninewells stands in it.
- hand: the Longwake Company's word for a Weaver in its employ.
- Harrier: the Calling of those who move; Storm and Tide, with Stone.
- Hearth: a Kindler who has learned to hold.
- Heddle: a Reweaver's workshop; also the heddle, the frame that holds a Pattern apart during the work.
- Heddle-master, Heddle-mistress: the master of a Heddle.
- Hedge, the: the living thorn wall of Ninewells, and the Compact's militia named for it.
- Hedge-captain: the commander of the Hedge; Jory Fenn.
- Hedgecrows, the: outlaws of the Hallows' hedgerows and barrows under Kit Tallow, who rob floss carts and know the Hobs.
- Hedgewright: a Holdfast who has taken up Bloom.
- Hem, the (sea): the warm southern sea along which Ilvary lies; the finished edge of the world.
- Hem, the (Glyph): a Glyph that shortens a Weave and finishes it clean.
- High Concord, the: the Concord's height, from about C 900 to C 1100, which the Ilvarene mourn.
- Hitch: a Mind Weave, one of the Binder's first.
- Hoarfrost: a Tide Weave, one of the Sower's first.
- Hob: a Hallows Tangle, a corner of the land gone strange; mostly harmless.
- Hobsmere: a Tide and Bloom Fount under a mere east of Ninewells; the classic first Holding, where the Compact teaches the Vigil.
- Hold: the Marcher form for a Holding, as in Ashdown Hold.
- Holder: whoever has bound a Fount.
- Holdfast: the Calling of those who stay; Stone and Lumen, with Ember.
- Holding: a Fount's bound Reach, shaped and ruled by its Holder.
- Hollins, Nance: Old Nance, senior Knotwife of Ninewells and the Compact's conscience.
- Hollow: a Sallows Snarl inside a hollow tree or drowned building, Bloom and Umbra.
- Hollowing: an Umbra Weave, one of the Shade's first.
- hollowman: one of the Tangled; a person whose insides have given way while the outside kept on.
- Hook, the: a Glyph that makes a Weave pull.
- Hospice, the: the Order of the Mended Thread's house at Alderheart, its seat in the Marches.
- Hospice-gate: a small Mender house in a town, where anyone is healed and nothing is asked.
- Hubris, the: the Chancery's explanation of the Unraveling: the Great Working tore the Loom.
- Hush, the: the eastern sub-region of the Weftmarches, Void and Mind, where the land goes quiet before the Last Stitch.

## I

- Ilvarene: the people of Ilvary.
- Ilvary: the southern land on the Hem coast, the old Concord heartland and the Chancery's own.

## K

- Kettle, the: an island of the Scatter.
- Kiln, the (Fount): an Ember Fount in the scarp face of the Emberfells, whose Welling is twine already spun.
- Kiln (Knot): an Emberfells Ember Tangle where a seam burns in the open.
- Kiln-camps: the Longwake Company's flosser camps in the Emberfells.
- Kilnwife, Kilnman: a Sower who has learned to Kindle.
- Kindle: an Ember Weave, one of the Kindler's first.
- Kindler: the Calling of those who start things; Ember and Storm, with Mind.
- Knot: a place where Threads tangled in the Unraveling and never settled; it turns, and is never the same twice.
- knot-prayer: a Marcher cord with a knot tied in it for a wish, thanks, fear or name, untied at the Selvage.
- Knotwife: a Marcher folk-weaver and midwife, licensed by nobody, who ties knots for those who cannot.

## L

- Ladder, the: a Glyph that builds a Weave up over time.
- Landmark: a fixed, deliberately made place that survived the Unraveling; it does not turn.
- Lantern: a Concord Lumen working that steadies the Patterns around it; a tower with a light that does not go out.
- Lantern Chancery, the: the restored Concord office of charters at Cassevault; the civil power of Ilvary.
- Lanterner: a sealed Weaver in Chancery service.
- Lanternmark: a Lumen Weave, one of the Binder's first.
- Lanternmere: the mere in the Sallows into which the Sunken Lantern sank.
- lantern-oak: a tree of the Sallows whose leaves hold light after dark; found nowhere else.
- Lantern-schools: the Chancery's schools of weaving in Ilvary.
- lantern-steel: a Concord material.
- Lanternward: a Lumen Weave, one of the Holdfast's first.
- lapse: what a binding does when its Holder dies or is too long absent; the Chancery issues writs of lapse.
- Last Stitch, the: a Mending work; the line of needles planted across the Fray's western edge in M 71, past which the Fray has not widened.
- Letting Go, the: the Marcher and Mender explanation of the Unraveling: the maker's hand let go of the Loom.
- Licensing Dispute, the: the cold war between the Chancery and the Compact over seals and the Vigil, since M 287.
- Long Sailing, the: the Wakefolk voyage west toward the Selvage in the Loose Years, from which few returned.
- Long Walk, the: the duckboard road through the Sallows.
- Longwake: a Wakefolk wake-house of the Scatter; the founding house of the Company.
- Longwake, Tomaru: Factor of the Longwake Company at Lowkeel, Varu's grandson.
- Longwake, Varu: the wake-house matriarch who chartered the Longwake Company in M 268.
- Longwake Company, the: the chartered trading consortium of Wakefolk wake-houses and the house of Orriaux, seated at Lowkeel; it harvests Knots.
- Loom, the: the whole of reality, in which everything is woven.
- Loomroad: a Concord paved road, lit by Lanterns; the one through the Marches is broken in places.
- Loomwright, the: the scholars' name for whoever set the Loom; absent.
- Loop, the: a Glyph that makes a Weave return.
- Loose Years, the: the first seventy years of the Mending Count, when few were writing.
- Loosed, the: those who went into the Fray and came back, who hold that the Loom should be let go; a movement, not an order.
- Loosening, the: the present quickening of Knots, Founts and Weavers, named by the Spindlewatch in M 301.
- loose-thread: what close folk call a Weaver, half in affection and half in warning.
- loose-wove: an item whose Pattern has room for Glyphs.
- Lowkeel: the Longwake Company's port on the Saltwrack, the largest settlement in the Marches after Ninewells; also a wake-house.
- Lowkeel, Seshe: a Wakefolk Weaver who left the Company and teaches the Shade's Calling at Ninewells.
- Lowkeel Eye: a Tide Fount under the Company's counting-house at Lowkeel, bound under charter with Writs that tax every cart.
- Lumen: the Thread of light and order.
- Lumenspan: the first Span of the year, when the light returns.
- Lumen-stone: the material of which the Last Stitch's needles are wrought.

## M

- Marcher: a person of the Weftmarches; anyone who came later and stayed.
- Marching, the: the era of the resettling of the Weftmarches, from about M 200 to the present.
- Marchwarden: the Chancellor sent to the Weftmarches; Isaure Castellane.
- Marrick, Isolde: Lumen-weaver of the Nine Who Stayed.
- Marrick Downs, the: the barrow-fields of the Hallows.
- Mend: a Bloom Weave, one of the Sower's first.
- Menders, the: the Order of the Mended Thread, as everyone calls them.
- Mending, the: the era after the Needle-rite, from M 71 to about M 200.
- Mending Count, the: the present calendar, written M, from the Selvage after the Unraveling.
- Mending Houses, the: the godless church of Ilvary, whose creed is the Rule of the Needle.
- Mending works: Landmarks made after the tear by people trying to hold the world together.
- Mind: the Thread of thought, will and the arcane; the Thread that knows it is a Thread.
- Mindspan: the eighth Span of the year, the long nights of talk.
- Mire's End: a drowned Concord town on the Saltwrack flats.
- Mirevault (city): the Tide-house's city on the Hem coast, where the Relighting began and the Mending Houses keep their mother-hospice.
- Mirevault (house): the Concord house of Tide, which kept its name through the Loose Years.
- Mirevault, Elouan: Stitcher and Warden of the Stitch, who has gone a day's walk into the Fray.
- Mirevault's Lantern: the first Lantern re-lit after the tear, in M 140.
- Moot, the: the Compact's assembly, which meets in the Wellhouse at Ninewells.
- Mooring: the Wakefolk form for a Holding, as in Greyheron Mooring.
- Mothkeeper: a Shade who has learned to Bind.
- mothhollow: one of the Tangled, of the Sallows.
- Mothlight: a Sallows Umbra Tangle where shadows begin to detach and the moths lead you in.
- Mothwell: an Umbra Fount in a Sallows clearing, unbound, which has taken three Vigils and given none back.

## N

- Needle, the (title): the head of a Mender house.
- Needle, the (Glyph): a Glyph that makes a Weave pierce.
- Needle-burnings, the: the persecution of Weavers in Ilvary during the Loose Years.
- Needle-rite, the: the working of M 71 by which Benoic Solenne's company planted the Last Stitch.
- Needle's Eye, the: a Void and Mind Fount at the foot of the Last Stitch's central needle, bound by no one.
- Nine Who Stayed, the: the nine Weavers who bound the Wellmother in common in M 226 and founded Ninewells.
- Ninewells: the town in the Hallows where new Weavers begin; the safest place in the Weftmarches.
- Ninth Void: the ninth day of Voidspan, the day of the Unraveling, on which no one weaves.
- Ninth Well, the: Void's well at Ninewells, which bears no name because its Weaver gave hers into the binding.

## O

- Old Reed: a drowned stead in the Sallows, built inside a lantern-oak before the tear.
- Old Reed Hollow: the best-known Hollow in the Sallows, in the lantern-oak at Old Reed.
- Ond: a Loosed Weaver who does not speak.
- Ondur: the Durn city delved into the Girdle's root behind Scarrow Gate.
- Orchard Snarl: a Hallows Bloom Snarl in a feral Concord orchard.
- Orchard Well, the: a waking Bloom Fount in the oldest feral orchard of the Hallows; its apples are prized and not safe.
- Order of the Mended Thread, the: the religious order of the Mending Houses, founded in M 74; the Menders.
- Orriaux: the Concord house of Umbra, which kept its name and is now a merchant house in the Longwake Company.
- Orriaux, Marisol: the house of Orriaux's agent at Lowkeel, who holds the Company's purse.

## P

- Pattern: any woven thing; its proportion of Threads and the way they are tied.
- Penrose, Gil: Watcher of the Spindlewatch, descendant of Oriel Penrose; teaches the Binder's Calling on market days.
- Penrose, Oriel: Mind-weaver of the Nine Who Stayed.
- plies: the layers of a Skein, which grow as a Weaver grows.
- Ply, the: a Glyph that strengthens a Weave.

## Q

- Quicken: a Bloom Weave, one of the Sower's first.
- Quiet Hand, the: the Marcher name for the Loomwright, who set the table and went out.
- Quiet Well, the: a Mind Fount in the Hush that answers only during the Selvage.
- Quillane: the Concord house of Void, the quiet house, which went quiet in the Loose Years and was not heard from again.

## R

- raveler: the Chancery's word for one of the Loosed.
- Reach (of a Fount): the land a Fount's Pattern reaches; bound, it is a Holding.
- Reach, the (movement): the first movement of weaving, sensing the Threads around you and in you.
- Read: a Mind Weave, one of the Binder's first.
- Reed, Tamsin: Tide-weaver of the Nine Who Stayed.
- Reed, the (Glyph): a Glyph that spaces a Weave evenly.
- Reedham: a village on the Sallows-edge of the Hallows, half Marcher and half Mender; Ash's home.
- Relighting, the: the Chancery's slow re-lighting of the Lanterns, begun at Mirevault in M 140.
- Remnant: a wrought servant of the Concord, still keeping a Pattern no one asked it to keep; roadwardens, seam-keepers, lanternbearers, doorkeepers.
- Reweaving, Reweaver: the craft of unpicking and re-binding the Threads in matter, and one who practises it.
- Ring, the: a Glyph that spreads a Weave.
- Rootstand: a Stone Weave, one of the Holdfast's first.
- Rouse: a Mind Weave, one of the Binder's first.
- Rule of the Needle, the: the Menders' creed: mend what you can; weave what you must; unpick nothing you cannot re-bind.

## S

- sag, the: frayed Stone; ground gone soft, walls that forget to stand.
- Sallows, the: the wet old forest sub-region west of the Hallows, Bloom and Umbra with Tide in the meres.
- Saltheart: a Tide Fount in a sea-cave under Greyheron Stack, held by the Greyheron wake-house.
- Saltwrack, the: the coastal sub-region of the Weftmarches, Tide and Storm with Void beneath; Lowkeel stands on it.
- Sapflow: a Bloom Weave, one of the Sower's first.
- Scarrow: the Durn delve-town at the Girdle's foot in the Emberfells.
- Scarrow Accord, the: the Concord's treaty with the Durn, C 388.
- Scarrow Gate: the iron door into the Girdle above Scarrow, opened in C 290, sealed in the Loose Years, reopened in M 184.
- Scatter, the: the archipelago in the Wake's eastern reach where the wake-houses moor.
- seal: the Lantern's seal, the Chancery's licence to a Weaver.
- sealed stead: a Marcher stead whose Holder has taken a Chancery charter; the Compact does not speak to them.
- Seam (Knot): a Hush Thinning where the Warp shows in the ground and a Weaver can see their own Skein from outside.
- Seam-master: the head of a Durn kin.
- seam-name: the second name a Durn takes at adulthood from the working where they came of age.
- Selvage, the (edge, days): the edge of the world, said to lie past the Wake; also the five days at the end of the year that belong to no Span.
- Selvage, the (Glyph): a Glyph that adds an ending to a Weave.
- Setting, the: the mythic era in which the Loomwright set the Loom and stepped away.
- Sev: a Loosed Weaver, once Severin Mirevault, a sealed Lanterner; the Loosed's voice, who spoke in the Wellhouse in M 294.
- Shade: the Calling of those who unpick; Umbra and Void, with Mind.
- Shadowstep: an Umbra Weave, one of the Shade's first.
- Shalesnare: a Stone Weave, one of the Harrier's first.
- Shoalfolk: the landsmen's name for the Wakefolk.
- shuttle (tool): the Reweaver's tool that carries new Thread in.
- Shuttle, the (sun): the Marcher name for the sun, which carries the day back and forth across the Warp.
- Shuttle, the (Glyph): a Glyph that passes a Weave from one target to the next.
- Sisters, the: tors in the Emberfells where the Storm Knots gather.
- Skein: a living person's Pattern; a Weaver's grows in Threads, ties and plies, and the game that came before this one was named for it; here it names the thing itself.
- Skyfall: an Emberfells Storm Thinning on a tor, where lightning stands in the air.
- Skyfold: a Storm Fount on the highest tor of the Emberfells, which woke in M 303 and is unbound; everyone wants it.
- Skystep: a Storm Weave, one of the Harrier's first.
- Slagtooth: a Durn seam-name and the site of the deepest Delve.
- slip: what a Snarl does when its Snag is cut; it collapses, sheds floss and begins to re-tangle.
- Slipknot, the: a Glyph that releases a Weave early.
- Slow, the: the Durn name for the Loomwright, who is in the stone and moves at its pace.
- Snag: the heart of a Snarl, where the tangle is tightest.
- Snarl: a Knot that has drawn hostile Patterns into itself; a dungeon.
- Solenne: the Concord house of Bloom, which kept its name through the Loose Years.
- Solenne, Benoic: the Bloom-weaver who led the Needle-rite in M 71 and planted the Last Stitch.
- Solenne, Oriane: the Needle of the Hospice at Alderheart, old, sharp and kind.
- Sower: the Calling of those who tend; Bloom and Tide, with Lumen.
- Span: one of the nine forty-day months of the year, each under a Thread, with a rising and a falling of twenty days.
- Speaker of the Moot: the presiding officer of the Compact; Maud Thatcher.
- Spindle, the: a Firstwork; a leaning doorless tower on Spindle Knap in the Hallows, whose shadow keeps the calendar.
- Spindle Knap: the hill north-west of Ninewells on which the Spindle stands.
- Spindlewatch, the: hermit-scholars at the Spindle who keep the count, measure the Warp's drift, and named the Loosening.
- Stillwater, the (nine days): the nine days after the Unraveling on which the Wake did not move; the Wakefolk do not sail on their anniversary.
- Stillwater (Knot): a Saltwrack Void Thinning, a patch of water that does not move.
- Stitcher: a full member of the Order of the Mended Thread; always a Weaver.
- Stone: the Thread of the physical and the earth, weight and endurance.
- stoneboar: one of the Tangled, a boar re-tied with Stone.
- Stonespan: the sixth Span of the year, harvest.
- stone-silence: the Durn reverence: not naming the maker, saying less than you know.
- Storm: the Thread of lightning and wind, change by movement.
- Stormspan: the third Span of the year, the spring winds.
- stripping: cutting a Knot's Snag and harvesting its floss before it can turn; a stripped Knot turns hungry.
- Sunken Lantern, the: a Concord Lantern in the Sallows that sank to its waist in Lanternmere when the Loom tore; its light burns green under the water.

## T

- Tallmast: a Wakefolk wake-house of the Longwake Company, and an island of the Scatter.
- Tallmast, Kelat: chief of the Company's flossers, who has cut more Snags than anyone alive.
- Tallow, Kit: Crowmother, leader of the Hedgecrows.
- Tangle: a Knot that is a wild pocket where the land has gone strange; dangerous mostly by accident.
- Tangled, the: creatures whose Patterns were caught in a Knot and re-tied.
- Teeth, the: an island of the Scatter.
- Thatcher, Maud: Speaker of the Moot at Ninewells, who holds the Compact together by will.
- Thing Beneath, the: the Wakefolk explanation of the Unraveling: something under the Loom surfaced or was let in.
- Thinning: a Knot where the Loom has worn thin rather than tangled; an anomaly where the rules bend.
- thornwight: one of the Tangled, of the Orchard Snarls.
- Thread: one of the nine strands from which every Pattern is woven; also the game's elements and damage types.
- Tide: the Thread of cold and water, patience and return.
- Tidespan: the second Span of the year, the thaw and the rains.
- Tithe of Salt, the: the Concord's treaty with the Wakefolk, C 410.
- turn: what a Knot does between visits; it re-tangles and is never the same twice.
- twine: floss spun stable enough to bind; named by Thread, as Tide twine.
- Twist, the: a Glyph that changes the Thread of a Weave.

## U

- Umbra: the Thread of shadow and decay, rest and endings; not evil and not Void.
- Umbraspan: the seventh Span of the year, leaf-fall.
- Unbinding: the counter-Vigil by which a challenger tries to fray a Holder's tie to a Fount and set their own.
- Undertow: a Tide Weave; also a Saltwrack Tide Snarl in a sea-cave that floods on its own tide.
- Unmade, the: what is left when a Pattern has been taken apart and did not stop moving; they come out of the Fray and are given no names.
- Unmaking: a Hush Void Snarl, a hole in the moor where the Loom shows through.
- Unname: a Void Weave, one of the Shade's first.
- unpicker: the Marcher word for a Shade, said with respect.
- Unraveling, the: the tearing of the Loom on Ninth Void, C 1211, whose true cause is not known.

## V

- Valcoure: the Concord house of Storm, which scattered into the hills; its name survives only on villages.
- Vaults, the: the Concord's archives beneath the cities of Ilvary.
- Vigil, the: the rite of binding a Fount; staying with it through its quiet hours until it takes you.
- Void: the Thread of chaos and unmaking, and the room the Loom was given.
- Voidspan: the ninth Span of the year, deep winter, in which the Unraveling fell.

## W

- Wake, the: the grey western ocean; the Wakefolk hold it to be the wake of the maker's leaving.
- wake-captain: a Company ship's master.
- wake-house: a Wakefolk boat-house and the lineage reckoned through it; a person's second name.
- Wakefolk: the people of the Wake and the Scatter, who give to the Wake and sing wakesongs.
- wakesong: a Wakefolk song that is both hymn and chart.
- Warden of the Stitch: a Mender who keeps the watch-house at the Last Stitch.
- Warp, the: the fixed threads of the Loom, seen as the rows of stars in the northern sky.
- Watcher: a member of the Spindlewatch; Gil Penrose leads them.
- watch-house, the: the Menders' post at the central needle of the Last Stitch.
- Wearing, the: the Durn explanation of the Unraveling: nothing caused it; cloth wears.
- Weave: a deliberate act of weaving, cast by a Weaver; a skill.
- Weaver: a person who can consciously alter a Pattern.
- Weft, the: the moving part of the Loom; the days, the weather, the lives of creatures.
- Weftmarch Compact, the: the league of Marcher steads sworn in the Wellhouse in M 241, which holds the Founts as a commons.
- Weftmarches, the: the frontier region where play begins, between the Girdle, the Wake, Ilvary and the Fray; the weft shows there.
- Welling: a bound Fount's yield, drawn by its Holder.
- Wellhouse, the: the round moot-hall over the Wellmother at Ninewells, where the Moot meets and the Compact was sworn.
- Wellmother, the: the Fount at the heart of Ninewells, bound in common by the Nine Who Stayed and renewed every Selvage; it cannot be contested.
- Wellsingers: the Weavers of the Whole Cloth, who sat at Founts and were told things.
- Wend, the: the river that runs from the Emberfells through the Hallows to the Wake.
- Wende, Hesper: the Marcher who mapped the Wend in M 212.
- Whole Cloth, the: the era before the Concord, when the Loom was whole and nothing turned.
- Whose Wake We Sail: the Wakefolk name for the Loomwright.
- Wrack: a Saltwrack Snarl, a drowned Concord ruin that surfaces at the Knot's low tide.
- Wrack of the Nine Oars, the: the Wrack the Company pays most for.
- Writ: a decree set by a Holder within a Holding, which the land itself helps enforce.
- wrought: raised from the land's own Pattern rather than built; also, of an item, rewoven in a Thread, as emberwrought.
