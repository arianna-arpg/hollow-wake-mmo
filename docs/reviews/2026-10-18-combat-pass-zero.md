# Combat pass zero: the grammar, the inventories and the instruments

Date: 2026-10-18. Author: the combat session (Claude). Base: main `a810b0a`. This record closes pass zero of the combat direction the owner set: depth over breadth, a grammar every mechanic obeys, the AI rebuilt as a player of it, and variety measured rather than asserted. It follows [the projectile corrections record](2026-10-18-projectile-corrections.md) and [the AI split record](2026-10-18-combat-ai-split.md), and it is the record the next passes cite for their baseline.

## What pass zero produced

- **The contract**: [docs/design/combat-grammar.md](../design/combat-grammar.md). Nine dimensions with their axes and units, the four operators, ten rules of composition and nine invariants each marked holds or target with its instrument and baseline, the playstyle matrix's rows, columns and coverage targets, what any AI in this engine must and must not be, the measurements the harness reports today and the ones the passes add with their gate order, how a new axis is added end to end, and what every combat change proves. No counts, nothing that goes stale; the findings live here. An Opus auditor reviewed the page unanchored on its author before this record closed; its twenty findings are folded into the page, and the ones that are gaps in the engine are listed below.
- **Three inventories**, kept with their probe and traces under [experiments/combat-grammar/pass0](../../experiments/combat-grammar/pass0/): the capability map (every handler field classified, from the engine's registrations, cross-checked by script against every `EffectSignature`), the AI inventory (every decision, read and write, measured with a replaying F# probe), and the design grammar (axes with prior art, the matrix, the counters, the AI literature, measurement, sources). Three independent Opus readers wrote them from the code, not from the port's drafts.
- **Instruments**, each its own request: the evaluation harness (`hollowwake tournament`, branch `claude/combat-evaluation-harness`, with the proving pairs and the standard duels moving from code into rules and a trace hash against a baseline), the morph test (branch `claude/combat-morph-test`), the composition linter (`hollowwake lint`, branch `claude/combat-composition-lint`), and the axis report (`weave --axes`, with the `AxisBindings` registry, branch `claude/combat-weave-axes`). Two seams that serve the grammar's rules landed or opened during the pass: validation that refuses unknown statuses, charges, patterns and filter words, with filter words as a registry (branch `claude/combat-effect-references`, rules 2 and 9), and held-mode blocks on presets (branch `claude/combat-port-12-held-presets`, rule 6).
- **The AI split record**, which claims this session's AI pieces and asks Codex to claim positioning.

## Findings

Numbers are from the inventories at `14f7f3b`; later merges change none of them.

**The engine's fields.** 48 handlers declare 349 signature parameters, 367 fields once blocks are split: 166 axes, 74 nodes, 95 choices, 32 flags. 48 handler fields read an attribute, as a fallback or a multiplier; 14 are literal numbers no expression can reach. The flags that hide axes are named one by one in the map's section 2, each with what it becomes: a lob is an arc height; the eleven `self`, `to` and `exclude_self` flags are one `who`; a field's `follow` and an aura's `toggle` are an anchor choice and the cast layer's toggle; `hit_once` and `rearm_on_exit` are a rehit lockout; `include_center` is a shared `spare` list that flights also need.

**Duplicated doors.** A radius is read in thirteen places with ten different defaults among them; an interval in ten, none an attribute; who-and-relation in five vocabularies; `delay` beside `fuse`, `aura` beside `field`, `segments` beside `cascade`. The chain effect and projectile chains read different ranges. The two layers of the `who` vocabulary disagree: a target kind `ally` includes the caster, a filter word `ally` excludes it.

**Frozen axes.** More than twenty rules a delivery reads (fork children and angle, chain range, homing radius, bounce, aftershock, seek, impulse) are rules no glyph can move. Every shipped flight writes its hit radius as a literal; there is no projectile size axis. Cast time, cooldown and every held-mode number live on forms only. Seventeen declared attributes (`projectile.spread`, `area.width`, `melee.reach`, `knockback`, `aim.spread`, `fuse.*`, `repeat.*`, `uses.*`, `guard.strength`, `cast.mobility`, `cooldown.added` among them) are moved by nothing shipped. The scaling law is copied into graphs: the weapon formula 95 times, the per-level term 119 times.

**A flight cannot see itself.** Hooks on a flight cannot read its speed, age, distance or resolved axes; a flight cannot spare the body it was born from; `on_expire` is a plain list, not a hook; forks have no budget, so each doubles the flights at every depth. `repeat` reruns on its first run's context with frozen time and distance. Depth restarts at follow-ups, status ticks and cast events, so a loop through them stops only on cooldowns, internal cooldowns and costs.

**Composition smells**, with file and line in the map's section 4: string switches on displacement kinds, intercept modes, mitigation words, pool names, ramp words, proc targets and who; choice words re-listed in a second switch that validates no filter; probes that parse raw nodes and duplicate handler semantics (one, `periodic_construct`, reads keys no signature declares and never holds); type checks where tags should gate (flights strike only hostile bodies, so none can seek an ally); `stunned` and `frozen` named in seven places of code; the runtime reading the form rather than the compiled weave in sixteen places, so no glyph or preset can grant a cast mode, a ramp, a guard, a timing or a toggle.

**Where the invariants fail today**, found by the audit of the contract: life falls outside the stage ladder through fall damage, a minion's decay, life costs and the `resource` effect, which lowers any resource of its target directly; the `spawn` effect places bodies with no per-owner cap and keeps them forever without a lifetime; toggles, contract summons, manual banks and brim floors do not end or decay on their own; sequels, follow-ups, metas and construct casts resolve without paying; the forecast plan covers primary candidates only, with no provider for projectiles and cascades; the delivery systems allocate nothing but the behavior and attribute budgets allow allocation; and request #20 adds a form field `release_check` whose `range` and `none` words omit line of sight or the check entirely, read from the form, against invariant 5 and rule 6.

**Found by the morph test**, on its first sweeps over the nine delivery families and three agency families (sixteen points each, and deep sweeps of five hundred and a thousand points a family that found nothing further): the `chain` probe reads only the attribute and never a node's `chains`, `flight:children` only `projectile.forks`, `construct:massed` only a node's `count`, `flight:spends` ignores a node's `rehit`, and `trajectory` misses negative kinematic attributes (the shipped `lava_glob` decelerates unseen), because the probes read one door of a mechanism that has two; `max_active` does not cap a single press, so one summon whose count exceeds it stands every body, against the minion contract; a move's `anchor` choice moves nothing, because the context's point is moved onto the struck body before the handler reads it; salvo beats are chained and rounded up a tick each, so a long salvo drifts late; the `resource` effect takes life with no hit at all and validation does not refuse it; and the two doors of an axis clamp differently (a count stops at twenty through the attribute and sixty-four on the node, a duration cannot be endless through the attribute). Each is pinned in the test's list of known findings, which fails when a finding stops reproducing, so the slice that fixes one removes it from the list.

**The AI.** One weave decision, a lottery over ready, affordable, in-range weaves; nothing reads `role`; 21 direct runtime writes and no intent path for any AI body, nine of the writes with no client equivalent (facing, a heal that adds life directly, a leash teleport, a trap's dismissal, a construct's raw effect run among them); the chooser ignores target requirements (the proving mower's twenty-two refused picks); channels never released or aimed, guards never parrying, charge-ups and overcharges always at full, no presses in timing windows, banks never reloaded, toggles never turned off, spends never waiting, corpses not perceived, no cancel at a dead foe; perception of sixteen scalars and nothing about threats, crowds, corpses or lines; no AI event. The detail and the measurements are in [the AI split record](2026-10-18-combat-ai-split.md).

**The matrix today.** 136 of 245 presets sit on `missile`, `swing` and `shot`. The least covered cells: guards and answers by causality (one glyph; nothing subscribes `parry`, `block`, `evade` or `guard_beat`; the `riposte` charge is gained by nothing), spreads and infects by coupling (`plague_rite` and the `contagion` glyph; `move_status` is unused), holds and releases as played (eleven exemplars, no AI competence). No archetype yet names allies as its targets.

**Exemplars.** The design grammar's section 2 names three prototype exemplars per archetype, picked for the axes they stress (`galvanic_reserve`, `flame_blast`, `living_bomb`, `contagion`, `command_assault`, `drowning_grasp`, `stone_rampart`, `riposte`, `time_dilation`, `iai_strike`, `righteous_fire` and their siblings). They are ported only where the matrix shows the gap, in passes two and three, each played by a doctrine.

**Baseline of the instrument.** The harness on main today, proving pairs, ten seeds: reaver against bulwark and hexer against plaguer are one-sided (0 and 1), pitfighter beats mower every time with the mower casting 0.45 of its kit, fletcher and evoker split 0.40 to 0.60; so the proving pairs already break the win-rate ceiling, and the gate until engagement is measured is breaking no band that was not broken. Mirrors are the harness's own check: over the eight proving patterns against themselves, twenty seeds each, every mirror but one splits its wins inside the interval of one half (reaver 0.40, hexer 0.35, plaguer 0.45, mower 0.45, fletcher 0.60, evoker 0.50 for the first side), the bulwark mirror draws every fight because two bulwarks cannot kill each other inside the cap, and the pitfighter mirror sits at the edge at twenty seeds (0.70 for the first side) and at one half at a hundred (0.53; the reaver mirror 0.50), so the arena favors no side (the reports are `mirrors.json` and `mirrors-100.json` in the evidence folder). A mirror that leaves the interval at a hundred seeds would mean the arena itself favors a side (the spawn or the first think), and the harness fixes that before any band is trusted. The morph test, the linter and the axis report state their first baselines in their own requests. The linter's first run over core corrected the capability map in one place: of the parameters the map listed as written by nothing, `on_first_hit`, `on_collide`, `amounts`, a projectile's `on_travel` and `on_expire` and an aura's `domain` are written by shipped content, and the map's one-use `gravity`, `pulses` and `sequence` are a rule, a channel flag and a crafting procedure rather than effect parameters; the rest of the map stands, and its string switches and form reads are found by the linter where its scan reaches (two switches and three form reads sit outside the directories it scans).

## Decisions taken for the passes

The design grammar's section 6 questions are the owner's. Until the owner answers, the passes proceed on these defaults, each reversible by content or by a flag on a command:

1. **What fight defines balance.** Both, banded separately: symmetric duels at equal level gate every slice, because the duel is the instrument's unit and is cheap and deterministic; scenarios against the world's packs, elites and a boss gate pass three's bands, because they are the loop the game is.
2. **What the AI may perceive.** Parity with the client by default: forecasts are its eyes, after an authored reaction delay, so telegraphs must be good; more only as a declared perception trait a doctrine names, so an elite's sharper senses are authored and telegraphed.
3. **Where difficulty lives.** In competence first (reaction, noise, commitment and risk as doctrine fields, every one an expression over the actor's attributes), in numbers second, banded apart.
4. **How many axes a glyph may move.** One freely, plus a price on payload or economy; a glyph that moves several is admitted only with its measurement in the record. This is composition rule 10 on the page.
5. **How a build's archetype is known.** Declared by tag and checked against the inferred one (which mechanics carried its damage in the harness); a mismatch is a lint finding.
6. **How recursion is bounded.** One causal budget per use, shared by triggers, procs, repeats and sequels, replaces the per-kind caps over the passes; until it lands the per-kind caps stand, and continuing the depth of the use through follow-ups, status ticks and cast events (composition rule 8) is the first step.
